About NIFES FUT Minna

The Nigeria Fellowship of Evangelical Students (NIFES) at the Federal University of Technology, Minna is an interdenominational fellowship committed to raising leaders who integrate Christian discipleship with academic excellence.

The NIFES Study Hub is an initiative of the NIFES Academic Commission, pooling verified past questions, exam simulations, and study tools to ensure every student has the resources they need to graduate with distinction.
