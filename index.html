Our Story

NIFES at FUT Minna

NIFES (Nigerian Fellowship of Evangelical Students) is an interdenominational Christian fellowship present on university campuses across Nigeria. At the Federal University of Technology, Minna, NIFES has been a home for students seeking to integrate faith with academic excellence.

The fellowship provides a community where students grow spiritually, build lasting friendships, and support one another through the rigours of engineering and technology education. The NIFES Study Hub is an extension of that mission — a digital library where students past and present pool their academic resources for the benefit of all.
