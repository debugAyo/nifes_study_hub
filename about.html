Our History & Mandate

NIFES at Federal University of Technology, Minna

The Nigeria Fellowship of Evangelical Students (NIFES) is an interdenominational Christian student movement present in tertiary institutions across Nigeria, affiliated globally with the International Fellowship of Evangelical Students (IFES).

At FUT Minna, spanning both the Gidan Kwano and Bosso campuses, NIFES has been a sanctuary of spiritual growth and intellectual diligence. We believe Christian scholars are called to be the head and not the tail — leading with distinction in engineering, pure and applied sciences, agriculture, and computing.

The NIFES Study Hub was birthed by the Academic Commission to address the challenges faced by students preparing for rigorous semester continuous assessments and computer-based tests.

Our Pillars

The Four Pillars of Academic Ministry

How the NIFES Academic Commission empowers students to excel semester after semester.

1. Spiritual Diligence

Approaching academic studies not merely as a degree requirement, but as an act of Christian stewardship and worship.

2. Verified Question Bank

Curating genuine FUT Minna examination archives with step-by-step verified workings so students understand foundational concepts.

3. Peer Tutoring

Senior scholars in 400L and 500L voluntarily conducting weekend tutorials and review sessions for 100L and 200L students.

4. First-Class Focus

Equipping students with CGPA planning tools and CBT mock test simulators to build speed, accuracy, and confidence.
